About ACEM Hostel

There are two separate hostels for boys and girls students accommodating about 200 students with in the campus. Both the hostels have necessary infrastructure and modern kitchen. The inmates are provided with a cot, a chair and a table. There is a cooperative store available in the hostel, to serve the day-to-day needs of the inmates. Hostel admission form is available in the hostel office for sale on payment.
